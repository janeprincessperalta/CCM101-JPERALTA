# **Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection**

## **Mission Reflection**

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission gave me a better understanding of the major cloud platforms. Among AWS, Azure, and GCP, I found Amazon Web Services (AWS) the most interesting. What caught my attention was the number of services available for different purposes, including computing, data storage, databases, and networking. I also realized that AWS can meet the needs of both small businesses and large organizations.

During the activity, I discovered that AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform share many common features. Each platform offers cloud-based computing, storage, networking, database, identity, and Kubernetes services. They also provide resources that can be adjusted according to the workload of an organization and have infrastructure available in different parts of the world.

One important lesson I learned is that selecting a cloud provider requires careful consideration of the organization's situation. Factors such as available budget, existing systems, security, application requirements, expected growth, and technical resources should all be evaluated. For instance, Azure may be more appropriate for an organization that already depends on Microsoft products, while GCP can be a strong option for projects involving AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes.

The role of a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a provider is more than simply picking the most well-known cloud platform. The decision should be based on what the client actually needs. This activity allowed me to practice examining different cloud services and matching them with specific business requirements.

This mission also helped me develop my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio. I organized my research on AWS, Azure, and GCP, prepared platform comparisons, recorded my recommendations for different clients, and documented the Linux activities I completed. Through this experience, I gained a clearer understanding of cloud computing and created a portfolio that reflects the technical knowledge and skills I have developed.
